Picability

Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026 · Aziz Hamza, Creator and Operator of Picability

1. Introduction

Picability is a social accountability application that helps users build habits, maintain streaks, and connect with friends. This policy describes how we collect, use, share, retain, and delete information.

2. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Usernames, email addresses, and securely hashed passwords.
  • Age eligibility: Date of birth submitted during registration to verify eligibility. We do not intentionally store the submitted birth date in the account profile.
  • Social activity: Friendships, requests, habit names, streaks, check-ins, comments, reactions, and visibility settings.
  • Private content: Messages and photos submitted through check-ins.
  • Safety information: Reports, explanations, and moderation evidence.
  • Notifications: Device or browser notification subscription information where enabled.
  • Technical information: Hosting providers may process IP addresses, request information, and diagnostic logs.

3. How We Use Information

We use information to operate accounts, friendships, habits, streaks, messaging, notifications, visibility controls, moderation, security, customer support, and legal compliance.

4. Disappearing Messages and Photos

Private check-in messages and photos are intended to disappear from the viewing interface after viewing. Once all intended recipients have viewed content, Picability is designed to delete the original stored content. Unopened content is subject to automatic deletion after approximately 30 days.

Disappearing from the interface does not necessarily mean immediate deletion from every storage system. Exceptions and circumstances where content or related information may remain include:

  • Other recipients: Content may remain until all intended recipients have viewed it or the retention period expires.
  • Reported messages: Reported text may be retained as moderation evidence after the original message disappears. Reports are subject to approximately 90-day automatic cleanup.
  • Reported photos: The original photo is designed to be deleted from active storage following successful report submission, although report metadata may remain.
  • Infrastructure backups: Deleted information may remain temporarily in provider-managed backups until they expire or are overwritten.
  • Operational records: Technical logs may retain limited identifiers, timestamps, and request information.
  • Legal requirements: Information may be preserved when legally required.
  • External copies: Recipients may capture screenshots, recordings, or other copies outside Picability's control.

These exceptions do not mean that Picability routinely retains copies of all viewed messages or photos.

5. Reports and Moderation

Users may report inappropriate content or behavior. Reported text may be retained as evidence. Moderation evidence may be cleared when a report is resolved. Reports are subject to automatic deletion after approximately 90 days in active application storage, subject to applicable legal requirements.

6. Sharing and Visibility

Habit and streak information may be shared with friends or displayed in the public feed according to applicable visibility settings. Private messages and photos are intended for designated recipients, subject to safety and reporting exceptions.

Service providers process information as necessary to host and operate Picability. Picability does not currently operate an advertising-based business model or intentionally sell personal information.

7. Data Retention

  • Viewed private content: Deleted according to recipient viewing and completion rules.
  • Unopened private content: Automatically deleted after approximately 30 days.
  • Moderation reports: Automatically deleted after approximately 90 days, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Account and streak information: Retained as needed to operate the service or until deletion, subject to applicable exceptions.

Removal from active storage does not guarantee immediate removal from backups or technical logs.

8. Account Deletion

Users may request account deletion through the application's account settings. The deletion process is designed to remove account information and associated friendships, streaks, and private content, subject to applicable exceptions.

For privacy requests or deletion assistance, contact picability.support@gmail.com.

9. Children and Teens

Picability is intended for users aged 13 and older. Children under 13 are not permitted to register. If we discover an account belonging to a child under 13, we will take appropriate action to remove it. Additional protections may apply to teenagers under applicable law.

10. Security

We use authentication, access controls, and other technical safeguards intended to protect information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

11. Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or request information about your personal data. Contact us to exercise applicable rights.

12. Changes

We may update this policy as Picability evolves. Updated versions will display a revised effective date, with additional notice when legally required.
